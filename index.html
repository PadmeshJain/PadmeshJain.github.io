Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Project Report?

A project report explains your business idea, the cost, how much funding you need and your expected income and expenses. Banks and government schemes use it to understand your project.

What is CMA Data?

CMA (Credit Monitoring Arrangement) data is a set of projected financial statements and working capital details. Banks ask for it when you apply for a working capital or term loan.

Can you prepare project reports for bank loans?

Yes. We prepare bank-ready project reports in the format your bank or scheme asks for. Loan approval is the decision of the bank.

Do you prepare agriculture project reports?

Yes. We prepare reports for projects like combine harvester, custom hiring centre, warehouse, cold storage, solar pump, rice mill and more. Eligibility and benefits depend on applicable scheme guidelines and lender approval.

Can I submit documents through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can send your KYC, quotations and financial details on WhatsApp. We will tell you exactly which documents are needed.

Do you provide ITR filing services?

Yes. We help individuals, professionals and businesses with Income Tax Return filing.

Do you provide GST registration and filing?

Yes. We help with GST registration, return filing and ongoing GST compliance.

Can you help with MSME/Udyam registration?

Yes. We assist you with the MSME / Udyam registration process.

Do you provide services across India?

Yes. We work online and on WhatsApp, so we can serve customers in any part of India.

How can I get a quotation?

Message us on WhatsApp or fill the form above. Once we understand your requirement, we will share the quotation.
